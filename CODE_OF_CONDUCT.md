# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free and welcoming
for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, focus on ideas rather than people, and assume good faith.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing private information are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to security@bna.dev. Reports will be handled
confidentially and action may include warning, removal of content, or a ban.
